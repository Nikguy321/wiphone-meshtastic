Pick the serial port that appears when you plug the phone in (usually CP210x, CH340 or usbserial).

If the phone doesn't show up

  1. Use a USB cable that carries data — many charging cables don't.
  2. Close anything else using the serial port (serial monitors, IDEs). An error like Failed to execute 'open' on 'SerialPort' means another program is holding the port right now.
  3. On Windows, install the CP210x or CH340 driver, then replug.
  4. Keep the phone powered on while flashing.

What this does

Your browser talks to the phone over USB (Web Serial) and writes the firmware directly — nothing to install, no Python, no command line. Settings, the phonebook and messages on the SD card are not touched. The page must stay open until it says it finished.
