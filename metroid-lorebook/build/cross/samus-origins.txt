CROSS-CLUSTER ISSUES:
1) ridley.json, 'Ridley: Ridley (overview and relationship with Samus)', third paragraph: the manga panic attack sits under 'During her first mission to Zebes...'. Per the manga, it happened during the Space Pirate takeover of Zebes while Samus served in the Federation Police (manga Vol 2 Ch 8; see the manga entries 'Samus's confrontation with Ridley and her trauma' and 'Mother Brain's betrayal of Zebes'). In the manga's account of her later bounty-hunter mission she held and brought him down with a single Missile. Suggested wording: 'Per the manga, when she met him again during the Pirate takeover of Zebes, while she served in the Federation Police, the sight of him triggered a panic attack and he beat her down.' Sources: https://www.metroidwiki.org/wiki/Ridley and the manga entries' citations.
2) ridley.json, overview and 'Ridley's appearance, abilities and intelligence': the claim that Ridley said he healed by eating the K-2L colonists, and 'taunted that her mother had been among them', is not on Metroid Wiki's Ridley or Psychosis pages. The manga editor left it out as unverifiable. Only Wikitroid search snippets support it, and they say he could have eaten her mother, not that she was among them. Soften it to that or drop it.
3) ridley.json overview: 'Ridley led the Space Pirate raid on ... K-2L, seeking its Afloraltite fuel'. Metroid Wiki's Ridley page backs this ('in pursuit of ... Afloraltite'), but its Samus Aran and Afloraltite pages, and the manga and samus-bio entries, say the Pirates followed the Chozo ship and only found the afloraltite after landing. Minor; suggest 'followed a Chozo ship there and seized its Afloraltite'.
4) dread.json 'Dread: The Chozo tribes - Thoha, Mawkin and Raven Beak's coup' nearly duplicates chozo.json 'Chozo: Chozo tribes – Thoha and Mawkin' (SR388 history, massacre, ZDR quarantine, DNA). Both fire on Thoha/Mawkin in ZDR talk. Consider trimming the dread entry to a ZDR-specific summary with a pointer by name, or tell the chozo cluster which one should be the home.
5) The motive differs between entries, though it is not strictly a contradiction. Chozo tribes says Raven Beak took Quiet Robe to ZDR 'intending to return to SR388 once the Metroids had wiped out the X' (Metroid Wiki Raven Beak page, verbatim). Dread: Raven Beak / Planet ZDR say he went to ZDR 'to raise Metroids there'. The dread editor should confirm, or give both.
6) build/index.txt still lists a stale federation draft, 'Galactic Federation: Federation Police and Army', with keys Federation Police, Jigrad, Kreatz, Mauk and Chief Hardy. The current federation.json entry is 'Federation Police and military command' and has none of the manga names. Regenerate the index.
7) Low priority: zebes.json 'Zebes: Samus's first Zebes mission (Zero Mission)' has the key 'fall of Zebes', which also sits on manga 'Mother Brain's betrayal of Zebes' (the actual fall of Zebes to the Pirates). The zebes entry already has 'Zebes has fallen' for the Pirate logs and could drop 'fall of Zebes'.
Verified as consistent, no action needed: the dread, fusion, metroids and zebes entries agree with the corrected Itorash order, the BSL defiance, Sector Zero, the baby Metroid and the Gray Voice details. The Fusion lines 'Samus, this is your last mission' and 'They lent me a hand' are confirmed by the retropixel transcript.
