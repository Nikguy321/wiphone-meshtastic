1) samus-bio, 'Samus: Samus Aran – raised by the Chozo on Zebes', last paragraph: 'When she returned years later as a bounty hunter, Mother Brain had turned against her makers, the Space Pirates held the planet...' This implies she first learned of the fall then. Per the manga, she came to Zebes with Kreatz and Mauk as a Federation Police officer during the Pirate takeover. There she confronted Mother Brain, was beaten by Ridley during a panic attack, saw Gray Voice die and escaped; only later did she leave the Federation and return as a bounty hunter. Suggested wording: 'She saw Zebes fall once already, as a Federation Police officer during the Pirate takeover (per the manga); when she returned years later as a bounty hunter, ...'. 2) dark-samus, 'Dark Samus: The return – Colossus, Phaaze and the Norion attack': 'raiders extracted Aurora Unit 313 and jettisoned the crew into space' is stated as fact. That is only the Pirates' own claim in the 'Taking Valhalla' log ('At the command of Dark Samus, we jettisoned the crew...'). Samus found many Marines' bodies aboard the wreck. It should be attributed to Pirate records, as prime3 'GFS Olympus and the Valhalla' already does. Sources: https://www.metroidwiki.org/wiki/G.F.S._Valhalla and https://www.metroidwiki.org/wiki/Space_Pirate_Data

The five castaways included an engineer (Myles MacKenzie) and an android (VUE-995), so 'Federation personnel' or 'Federation companions' is more accurate (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metroid_Prime_4:_Beyond). Minor.
